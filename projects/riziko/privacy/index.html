Riziko Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: Riziko sends anonymous play data only if you say yes, and you can turn it off any time in Options → Share Play Data. The leaderboard and online matches use a few online services, listed below. Your settings and saved runs stay on your device.

Who we are

Riziko is made by an independent developer, [DEVELOPER NAME]. For any question about your data, or to ask for it to be deleted, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

Play data (only if you agree)

The first time you open the game, it asks whether it may send anonymous data about how you play the Rizikoway mode. Nothing is sent before you answer yes. You can change your answer any time in Options → Share Play Data.

If you agree, the game sends:

  • runs: when a run starts and ends, how far it got, the Jokers held, the time played;
  • rounds and boss fights: the result, the target and score, hands and discards used, the boss rule;
  • hands and discards: the hand type, the score, how close the play was to the best play the hand had, which Jokers fired, which helper buttons were used, thinking time, and why a discard was sloppy;
  • the shop, the map and rewards: offers, purchases, sales, rerolls, the dot chosen and the reward picked;
  • the first time you use a feature (for example the sort buttons or the deck window).

Each run gets a random id. The data contains no name, no email and nothing you type. It is never collected in the tutorial or in versus matches.

Why: to see which parts of the game are too hard or too easy, and to balance it.

Who processes it: Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies. Unity also records technical details with each event, such as the device model, operating system, game version, country (from the network) and a random install id. See Unity's privacy policy for game players. If you say no, the game also tells Unity that you did not agree.

Leaderboard

When you submit a score, the game sends it to PlayFab, a Microsoft service, together with the name you choose (or your Steam name), the round you reached and the run's seed. Other players can see these on the leaderboard. To keep your place on the board, the game creates a random player id on your device. See Microsoft's privacy statement.

Online versus matches

Online matches use Unity's multiplayer services, which sign you in with an anonymous id and connect you to the other player. During a match the game sends your moves, your player name and your portrait to your opponent. They are not stored after the match.

Steam

On Steam the game shows your Steam name and avatar and may use your Steam language. Steam's own privacy policy applies to your Steam account.

On your device

Settings, your profile, unlocked Jokers, boss cards and saved runs are stored only on your device (or in your browser's storage when you play on the web). Removing the game or clearing the browser's site data deletes them.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn play data on or off any time in Options → Share Play Data.
  • Ask us which data we hold about you, or ask for it to be deleted, at the contact address above.
  • If you live in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Riziko does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Play data is anonymous and only sent after a yes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
